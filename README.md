## Iron Pilgrim

A dieselpunk horror strategy game that runs in the browser.

It's 1931. Two years ago every clock on the continent stopped at 11:07, and the land went wrong. You're the new conductor of the Iron Pilgrim, an armoured diesel train making the last run across the Blight to Sallow Haven.

**How it plays**

- **Route map:** pick your path across three regions while the Quiet closes in behind you.
- **Resources:** manage fuel, hull, provisions, ward oil and lore.
- **Boardings:** turn-based fights along the train's cars. Every enemy shows its next move.
- **Crew:** six characters, each with their own secrets.
- **Runs:** each one is hard and you'll lose. What you learn carries over to the next.
- **Endings:** there are four.

**Running it:** open `iron-pilgrim/index.html` in a browser, with sound on. Progress saves in the browser.
